# Chamber Service evaluation

For the participant-facing protocol and service startup, use the repository [README](../README.md#participant-policy-interface).

The task has a maximum of 13 points: open the chamber (2), extract the old part (1), place it in the box (1), pick up the sponge and wipe five chamber faces (6), remove and place the replacement part (2), and close the chamber (1). Each scoring point is credited at most once per episode, and stages are enabled in order.
